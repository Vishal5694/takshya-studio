The Crafting Journey

From Nature To Light

01

Wood Selection

We handpick sustainably sourced timber, selecting pieces with compelling organic characteristics and natural live edges.

02

Sculpting & Shaping

Master artisans carve and shape the wood, respecting its natural form while crafting an elegant architectural silhouette.

03

Surface Finishing

Meticulous sanding and the application of natural oils enhance the grain, creating a rich, tactile surface.

04

Lighting Integration

Premium LED technology is seamlessly embedded into the wood, ensuring a soft, diffused, and cinematic glow.

05

Hand Assembly

Every component is carefully assembled by hand, ensuring flawless joinery and structural integrity.

06

Final Illumination

Rigorous testing of light temperature, spread, and ambiance to guarantee the perfect atmospheric luxury.
